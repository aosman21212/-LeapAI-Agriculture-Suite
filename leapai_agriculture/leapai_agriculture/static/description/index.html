About This Module

LeapAI Agriculture Suite brings farm, field and crop planning management into Odoo 19. Draw field boundaries on an interactive OpenStreetMap, track soil samples over time, plan crops per season, and log field activities — land preparation, sowing, irrigation, fertilization, spraying, weeding and harvest — that consume real warehouse stock, employee labour hours and machinery hours.

Every crop plan carries a live job cost sheet: budget vs. actual cost, cost per hectare, computed directly from completed field activities. This is phase 1 of the suite — monitoring & analytics (scouting, weather), livestock management and the contract-farming portal are planned for later phases.
